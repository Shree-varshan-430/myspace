# My Space SEO Copywriting & Content Style Guide

## My Space Engineering, Construction & Valuers

**Document purpose:** Define how My Space website copy should be written, optimized, reviewed, and maintained for search visibility, trust, and qualified enquiries.  
**Primary market:** Bengaluru / Bangalore  
**Primary objective:** Turn relevant local search visits into confident construction, design, interior, commercial, and valuation enquiries.  
**Document status:** Copywriting and editorial standard

---

## 1. Copywriting Strategy

My Space copy must solve a visitor problem before it promotes a company capability. People searching for construction, interior, design, or valuation services are often uncertain about where to begin, what to expect, what to prepare, and whom to trust.

The copy should make the visitor feel:

> **“This company understands what can be confusing and explains the next step clearly.”**

### 1.1 Core copy model

Every important page and section should follow this sequence:

> **Audience concern → Clear explanation → Process → Evidence → Limitation where relevant → Next step**

### 1.2 Primary positioning

My Space is an engineering-led partner for property owners and decision-makers in Bengaluru who need help planning, designing, building, visualizing, furnishing, or valuing a space.

### 1.3 Brand promise

> **Build Your Own Space**

Use this as the brand promise and campaign language. It should not replace descriptive service copy or the page’s search-focused H1.

## 2. Brand Voice

### 2.1 Voice characteristics

| Voice quality | Copy behavior |
|---|---|
| Clear | State the service and outcome directly |
| Calm | Reduce uncertainty instead of creating urgency or fear |
| Technical but accessible | Explain specialist terms in plain language |
| Confident | Make clear recommendations without unsupported superlatives |
| Warm | Acknowledge the personal or business importance of the space |
| Transparent | Explain scope, assumptions, limitations, and next steps |
| Local | Use Bengaluru context where it is genuine and useful |

### 2.2 Voice examples

| Avoid | Prefer |
|---|---|
| We make your dreams come true. | We help you plan, visualize, and build your space with clearer decisions. |
| We are the best construction company in Bangalore. | We provide residential and commercial construction services in Bangalore with a visible process. |
| Quality is our promise. | We explain scope, materials, stages, and review points before execution. |
| Get your loan approved fast. | Begin a property valuation enquiry for bank documentation, subject to the requesting institution’s requirements. |
| We provide world-class solutions. | We coordinate design, construction, interiors, and visualization around the needs of your property. |
| Contact us today before it is too late. | Tell us what you are planning, even if you are still working through the details. |

### 2.3 Emotional outcome

The copy should create reassurance through clarity. It should not rely on exaggerated aspiration, fear, false scarcity, or unverified authority.

## 3. Language and Terminology Rules

### 3.1 Bangalore and Bengaluru

Use **Bangalore** in selected search-facing elements where it aligns with the target keyword, including title tags, selected H1 or H2 wording, URLs where approved, and relevant service headings.

Use **Bengaluru** naturally in body copy, local context, business information, and editorial writing. Do not force both forms into every paragraph.

### 3.2 Construction terminology

Explain specialized terms at first use when the audience may not know them. For example:

> **Elevation design** is the visual and material study of a building’s exterior façade.

> **3D floor plan** is a visual representation of a layout that helps a client understand rooms, furniture, circulation, and spatial relationships.

> **Turnkey construction** generally refers to a coordinated delivery model from planning or design through execution and handover, but the exact scope must be defined by the engagement.

### 3.3 Valuation terminology

Use precise distinctions:

| Term | Meaning in website copy |
|---|---|
| Property valuation | An assessment of a property’s value for a defined purpose and scope |
| Valuation report | A formal document whose format and authority depend on the responsible professional and intended use |
| Market opinion | An opinion or estimate that may not carry the same scope as a formal valuation report |
| Construction estimate | An estimate of project work and cost; not a property valuation |
| Title verification | Legal review of ownership or title; not automatically included in valuation |

Do not use “bank approved property valuer,” “registered valuer,” “chartered engineer valuer,” or “approved valuer” unless current credentials and authorization are verified.

## 4. SEO Copywriting Principles

### 4.1 Search intent before keyword placement

Write for the query behind the keyword. A person searching **house construction company in Bangalore** wants to understand capability, process, scope, trust, and next steps. Repeating the phrase without answering those concerns does not create useful content.

### 4.2 One primary topic per page

Each page should have one primary keyword family and a clear purpose.

| Page | Primary keyword family |
|---|---|
| Homepage | construction company in Bangalore |
| Residential construction | house construction company in Bangalore |
| Commercial construction | commercial construction company in Bangalore |
| Civil construction | civil contractors in Bangalore |
| Interior design | interior design company in Bangalore |
| Elevation design | 3D elevation design Bangalore |
| 3D floor plans | 3D floor plan design Bangalore |
| Property valuation | property valuation in Bangalore |

### 4.3 Natural keyword use

Use keywords in:

- Title tag.
- H1 or an accurate equivalent visible heading.
- Introduction.
- One or more relevant subheadings.
- Image alternative text where descriptive.
- Internal links.
- FAQ questions where genuinely useful.
- Metadata.

Do not force exact-match keywords into every paragraph. Use natural variants such as **home construction**, **residential building**, **Bengaluru construction**, **interior design and execution**, **elevation**, and **property valuation enquiry**.

### 4.4 People-first content

The copy should be original, useful, current, and written for the visitor. Search engines may use headings and page content to understand a page, but rankings cannot be guaranteed through keyword placement alone.[1]

### 4.5 Local relevance

Mention Bangalore or Bengaluru when it helps the user understand the service area, local process, property context, or project relevance. Do not add neighbourhood names merely to create doorway pages.

## 5. Page-Level Copy Formula

Every core service page should contain the following content blocks.

### 5.1 Hero formula

**Eyebrow:** Short category label.  
**H1:** Visitor concern or clear service outcome plus location where appropriate.  
**Supporting paragraph:** What My Space helps with, for whom, and why the process is useful.  
**Primary CTA:** Relevant next action.  
**Secondary CTA:** View process, work, or preparation guide.

### 5.2 Service explanation formula

1. Identify the common concern.
2. Define the service in plain language.
3. State what My Space can help coordinate or deliver.
4. State what depends on the project or professional scope.
5. Link to the next step.

### 5.3 Proof formula

Use one or more of:

- Approved project case study.
- Drawing or elevation.
- Construction progress.
- Material detail.
- Team qualification.
- Process evidence.
- Verified testimonial.
- Clear contact and business information.

### 5.4 CTA formula

The CTA should answer: **What should the visitor do next, and what should they prepare?**

Example:

> **Discuss Your Home Project**  
> Share your site location, property type, approximate area, and what is currently unclear.

## 8. Cost and Estimate Copywriting

Cost content is valuable because visitors often research price before contacting a provider. It is also high-risk if the website publishes unsupported or outdated numbers.

### 8.1 Cost page structure

1. Give a short, qualified answer.
2. State that cost depends on named variables.
3. Explain what may be included.
4. Explain what may be excluded.
5. Show the date and assumptions.
6. Give examples only when they are verified.
7. Link to a consultation or estimate discussion.

### 8.2 Recommended wording

> Construction cost in Bangalore varies with built-up area, site access, structural complexity, specification, finish level, services, and project scope. A useful estimate should state its assumptions rather than present one universal number.

### 8.3 Avoid

- “Lowest construction cost guaranteed.”
- “Fixed price for every home.”
- A number without date, specification, tax, and exclusions.
- A calculator that appears to produce a binding quotation.
- Competitor price comparisons without current and comparable evidence.

### 8.4 Cost CTA

> **Discuss Your Project Scope**  
> Share the site location, approximate area, property type, and specification direction to begin a more useful conversation.

## 9. Valuation Copywriting and Compliance

### 9.1 Required distinctions

Copy must clearly separate:

- Property valuation.
- Construction estimate.
- Market opinion.
- Legal title verification.
- Bank or lender decision.

### 9.2 Approved language pattern

Use:

> Property valuation enquiries for bank documentation, property decisions, sale or purchase, insurance, tax, inheritance, or other defined purposes, subject to the responsible professional’s scope and requirements.

Do not use:

> Get your loan approved with our valuation.

### 9.3 Qualification-dependent language

Use the following only after verification:

- Registered valuer.
- Chartered engineer valuer.
- Bank-approved property valuer.
- Approved valuer list.
- Certified valuation report.

The website should identify the responsible professional and relevant scope where a formal claim is made.

## 10. Metadata and Heading Guidelines

### 10.1 Title tag rules

A title tag should be unique, accurate, concise, and include the page topic and location where useful.

**Good:** `House Construction Company in Bangalore | My Space`

**Weak:** `Best Construction Company | Quality Builders | Home Solutions`

### 10.2 H1 rules

- Use one H1 per page.
- Make it understandable without the title tag.
- Reflect the visitor’s concern or service.
- Include location naturally where useful.
- Do not use unsupported superlatives.

### 10.3 H2 rules

H2s should answer questions or organize decisions. Examples:

- What should happen before construction begins?
- What can you see before execution?
- What should you prepare?
- How does the valuation process work?
- What is included in the scope?

### 10.4 Meta description rules

A meta description should:

- Explain the page’s value.
- Include the primary topic naturally.
- Mention Bangalore or Bengaluru when relevant.
- Provide a reason to click.
- Avoid guarantees and exaggerated claims.

## 12. Image, Caption, and Alt-Text Copy

### 12.1 Image selection principle

Images must explain or support the content. The website should use:

- Architectural drawings.
- Floor plans.
- Elevation studies.
- Construction progress.
- Material details.
- Completed residential spaces.
- Commercial spaces.
- Interiors.
- Greenery and landscape.
- Property inspection or report context for valuation.

### 12.2 Alt-text examples

| Image | Recommended alt text |
|---|---|
| Home exterior | Contemporary Bengaluru residence with stone façade and landscaped courtyard |
| Construction progress | Residential construction site showing reinforced concrete structure and masonry work |
| Interior | Warm residential interior with wood storage, natural light, and garden view |
| Elevation drawing | 3D elevation study showing façade materials and window proportions |
| Floor plan | Furnished 3D floor plan showing living, dining, kitchen, and bedroom relationships |
| Valuation property | Residential property exterior photographed for a valuation inspection context |

Do not write alt text such as `best construction company Bangalore image`.

### 12.3 Captions

Use captions to identify project category, status, location, or visual purpose. Example:

> **Residential elevation concept — Bengaluru.** Visualization for design discussion; not a final approval drawing.

## 13. FAQ Copywriting

FAQ answers should be direct, complete, and short enough for scanning. The first sentence should answer the question. Add a link when a longer explanation is needed.

### Example

**Question:** I am not sure where to start. Can I still contact My Space?

**Answer:** Yes. You can begin with your property location, project type, and what is currently unclear. My Space can use the first conversation to identify the relevant service and next step.

### Do not use FAQs to:

- Repeat the same keyword unnaturally.
- Add hidden promotional claims.
- Make legal, financial, or professional promises.
- Substitute for a proper service page.

## 14. Calls to Action and Microcopy

### 14.1 CTA hierarchy

| Level | CTA | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | Tell Us What You Are Planning | Broad homepage conversion |
| Primary | Discuss Your Home Project | Residential intent |
| Primary | Discuss a Commercial Project | Commercial intent |
| Primary | Plan Your Interiors | Interior intent |
| Primary | Request a Design Consultation | Elevation and 3D intent |
| Primary | Request a Valuation Consultation | Valuation intent |
| Secondary | See How It Works | Low-commitment trust action |
| Secondary | View Project | Proof exploration |
| Utility | Call / WhatsApp | Direct contact |

### 14.2 Form labels

Use conversational but clear labels:

- `What do you need help with?`
- `Where is the project located?`
- `Tell us what you are planning`
- `What stage are you at?`
- `What would you like to understand first?`

### 14.3 Error and success microcopy

**Required field error:** `Please tell us what you need help with.`

**Invalid phone error:** `Enter a valid phone number with country and area code.`

**Success:** `Thank you. We have received your enquiry. You do not need to have every detail ready; our team will review it and contact you through your preferred method.`

**Spam or technical failure:** `We could not send the enquiry at the moment. Please try again or contact us by phone or WhatsApp.`

## 15. Local SEO Copy Rules

### 15.1 Service-area language

Use accurate statements such as:

> Serving Bengaluru and nearby areas, subject to project scope and availability.

Do not list every neighbourhood unless the company genuinely serves it and can provide useful local information.

### 15.2 Local context opportunities

Use local context in:

- Construction cost variables.
- Site access and logistics.
- Local property types.
- Climate and material considerations where professionally relevant.
- Approval or documentation context only when verified.
- Project case studies.
- Service-area pages with unique evidence.

### 15.3 Business information consistency

The formal name, phone, address, hours, URL, and service area must be consistent across the site and approved local listings.

## 16. Editorial Workflow

### 16.1 Article workflow

1. Select a real audience question.
2. Confirm search intent and target keyword family.
3. Define the answer in one sentence.
4. Create the outline.
5. Gather approved facts, examples, and images.
6. Draft in plain language.
7. Review professional, cost, or valuation claims.
8. Add metadata and internal links.
9. Complete accessibility and readability review.
10. Publish and record update ownership.

### 16.2 Required reviewers

| Content type | Required review |
|---|---|
| General service page | Business owner or service lead |
| Construction process | Construction or engineering lead |
| Cost content | Business owner and person responsible for estimates |
| Valuation content | Responsible valuation professional or qualified reviewer |
| Legal and privacy content | Appropriate legal or policy reviewer |
| Project story | Client permission and business owner approval |

### 16.3 Update schedule

Review cost, valuation, regulatory, service, and local information at least quarterly or whenever the underlying facts change. Review evergreen service pages at least twice per year.

## 17. Copy QA Checklist

Before publication, confirm:

- The page has one audience and one primary intent.
- The H1 is clear, useful, and accurate.
- The primary keyword appears naturally.
- The first paragraph answers the visitor’s likely question.
- The copy explains what My Space does and what depends on project scope.
- The process is visible.
- The page includes proof or a transparent limitation.
- The CTA matches the visitor’s intent.
- Keywords are not repeated mechanically.
- Bangalore/Bengaluru usage is natural.
- Cost claims include date, assumptions, exclusions, and update owner.
- Valuation copy does not imply loan approval.
- Professional claims are verified.
- Project images and testimonials have permission.
- Meta title and description are unique.
- Internal links are relevant.
- Alt text describes the image rather than stuffing keywords.
- Copy is readable on mobile.
- Forms and microcopy are clear.
- The page has an owner for future updates.

## 19. Final Copy Recommendation

My Space should write as a **guide for important property decisions**, not as a company trying to sound impressive.

The copy should consistently answer:

> **What is the visitor trying to do? What may be difficult? How can My Space help? What should happen next? What are the limits of the service?**

The strongest editorial formula is:

> **Clear problem → Practical explanation → Visible process → Approved proof → Honest limitation → Simple next step.**
