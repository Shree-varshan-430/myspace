# My Space SEO Keyword Strategy

## Based on the supplied keyword list

**Business:** My Space Engineering, Construction & Valuers  
**Market:** Bangalore / Bengaluru  
**Primary objective:** Convert high-intent local searches into qualified construction, design, interior, and valuation enquiries.

---

## 1. Overall Assessment

The supplied keyword list is a strong strategic starting point because it covers the complete audience journey: discovery, service comparison, cost research, process questions, local intent, and valuation requirements.

Its biggest strength is the separation of intent across distinct service areas. Residential construction, commercial construction, interiors, visualization, civil construction, and property valuation should not be forced into one generic page. Each needs its own focused landing page supported by relevant FAQs and useful content.

The list should be treated as a **keyword and intent framework**, not as copy to insert mechanically. The website should use one primary topic per page, natural keyword variations, clear service language, and evidence-led content.

There is no guarantee that a particular keyword or H1 will rank highly. Search performance will depend on content quality, local prominence, technical SEO, authority, competition, business proof, and the quality of the user experience.

## 5. Compliance-Sensitive Keywords

Some keywords may have strong commercial intent but must not be used as factual claims unless My Space has verified qualifications, registrations, authorization, and current scope.

| Keyword or claim | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Best construction company in Bangalore | Do not use as a factual claim. Use “construction company in Bangalore” and prove quality through evidence. |
| BBMP approved construction company Bangalore | Use only if the exact approval, role, and current status are verified. Approval may apply to a project or drawing, not the company generally. |
| Bank approved property valuer Bangalore | Use only with verified empanelment or authorization for the relevant institution. |
| Registered valuer Bangalore | Use only if the responsible professional has the applicable registration and the website identifies the scope accurately. |
| Approved valuer list Bangalore | Do not imply inclusion in a list without current proof. |
| Chartered engineer valuer Bangalore | Use only if the relevant professional qualification and valuation authority are confirmed. |
| Fixed cost construction Bangalore | Use only when the scope, specification, exclusions, change process, and contract support the claim. |
| Guaranteed loan approval | Never use. A valuation does not control a bank’s decision. |

These terms may be addressed through careful educational content where appropriate, but the website must not optimize for a term by making a misleading claim.

## 8. Local SEO Implementation

The following business information should remain consistent across the website and approved local listings:

- Formal business name
- Physical address or accurate service-area description
- Phone number with country code
- Business hours
- Website URL
- Service categories
- Social profile links

Implement accurate structured data for the organization or most specific suitable local-business type. Include only information visible on the website and verified by the business.

Create neighbourhood or area pages only when My Space has genuine service coverage and unique content for that area. Do not create thin pages that swap only the neighbourhood name.

---

## 9. Keyword Quality-Control Rules

Before publishing a page, confirm:

1. The page has one primary search intent.
2. The primary keyword appears naturally in the title, H1 or equivalent visible heading, introduction, URL where appropriate, and at least one internal link.
3. The page answers the visitor’s actual questions rather than repeating keywords.
4. The copy includes genuine local relevance.
5. Any price or process claim includes assumptions and limitations.
6. Any professional or bank-related claim has been verified.
7. The page uses unique content and original project evidence where possible.
8. The CTA matches the visitor’s intent.
9. The page has relevant image alternative text without keyword stuffing.
10. The content is reviewed and dated when it contains cost, regulatory, valuation, or process information that may change.

## Final Recommendation

Use the supplied keyword list as the foundation for a **hub-and-spoke local SEO structure**:

> **Homepage for broad integrated intent → focused service pages for transactional intent → useful articles and FAQs for cost, process, and decision-support intent.**

The most valuable differentiation is the combination of **construction, design, visualization, interiors, and valuation enquiries** under one clear brand. That combination should be expressed naturally across the homepage and reinforced with dedicated pages rather than repeated as keyword-heavy copy.
