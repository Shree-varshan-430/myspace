# My Space Website Content Matrix

## My Space Engineering, Construction & Valuers

**Document purpose:** Define the content, SEO focus, audience need, page structure, proof requirements, and conversion path for every priority page of the My Space website.  
**Business:** My Space Engineering, Construction & Valuers  
**Market:** Bengaluru / Bangalore  
**Brand promise:** Build Your Own Space  
**Primary website goal:** Generate qualified construction, design, interior, commercial, and property valuation enquiries.

---

## 1. How to Use This Matrix

This document connects four essential website layers:

1. **Audience need:** What the visitor is trying to solve.
2. **Search intent:** What the visitor is likely to search for.
3. **Page content:** What the page must explain and prove.
4. **Conversion path:** What the visitor should do next.

Each page should have one primary search intent and one primary conversion action. Secondary keywords can support the page, but they should not make the content repetitive or unfocused.

The content team should use this matrix as a writing brief. The design team should use it to determine section hierarchy, visual proof, image selection, and CTA placement. The development team should use it to create reusable page templates and content fields.

## 2. Global Content Rules

### 2.1 Voice and tone

The brand voice should be **clear, calm, technically credible, and human**. It should explain construction and valuation topics in plain language without sounding casual, exaggerated, or overly legalistic.

Use:

- Direct sentences.
- Specific outcomes.
- Visible process steps.
- Accurate limitations.
- Helpful explanations.
- Bengaluru-local context where genuine.

Avoid:

- “We make dreams come true.”
- “Best in Bangalore.”
- “Quality guaranteed” without a defined guarantee.
- “No. 1,” “leading,” or “trusted by thousands” without proof.
- Generic filler such as “We are committed to excellence.”
- Unverified project counts, years, prices, ratings, approvals, or bank relationships.

### 2.2 Default CTA language

| Intent | Recommended CTA |
|---|---|
| General project | Tell Us What You Are Planning |
| Residential construction | Discuss Your Home Project |
| Commercial construction | Discuss a Commercial Project |
| Civil construction | Discuss Civil Construction |
| Interior design | Plan Your Interiors |
| Elevation and 3D plans | Request a Design Consultation |
| Valuation | Request a Valuation Consultation |
| Low-commitment visitor | Start With What You Know |
| Direct contact | Call My Space / WhatsApp My Space |

Do not use **Submit** as the primary lead-form button.

### 2.3 Required content approval

The business owner must approve all published claims relating to qualifications, licences, bank empanelment, statutory approvals, project counts, years of experience, project costs, timelines, warranties, testimonials, client logos, and completed project images.

## 3. Global SEO and Metadata Standards

Every indexable page must have:

- One clear H1.
- Unique title tag.
- Unique meta description.
- Descriptive URL.
- Canonical URL.
- Breadcrumbs where useful.
- Relevant internal links.
- Descriptive image alternative text.
- Accurate structured data where applicable.
- A clear primary CTA.

### Recommended metadata pattern

**Title format:** `Primary service or topic | My Space Bangalore`

**Meta description format:** `Explain the service, location, audience outcome, and next step in approximately one or two concise sentences.`

**Image alt-text format:** Describe what is visible and relevant to the page. Do not stuff keywords. Example: `Contemporary residential elevation with stone and glass façade in Bengaluru`.

### 5.4 Homepage proof requirements

The homepage should contain at least three proof types before the final CTA:

1. **Process proof:** A visible, understandable journey.
2. **Visual proof:** Drawings, plans, materials, construction progress, and finished spaces.
3. **Business proof:** Approved project evidence, team credentials, verified testimonials, or transparent contact information.

If project statistics and testimonials are not yet available, use process and design proof instead of invented evidence.

| Service selector | Six relevant images or drawings | Do not use one generic image across all cards |
| Process | Five stage visuals | Sketch, plan, elevation, construction, completed space |
| Projects | Original project images and drawings | Label Completed, Ongoing, or Concept |
| Materials | Concrete, brick, stone, wood, metal, glass, greenery | Use as tactile editorial detail |
| Elevation page | Elevation sketch, render, material study | Distinguish visualization from approval drawing |
| 3D floor plan page | 2D plan and 3D furnished plan | Show spatial understanding clearly |
| Valuation page | Property exterior, inspection, report context | Avoid bank branding unless authorized |
| About page | Team and working context | Use approved portraits and credentials |
| Contact page | Office or service-area image if useful | Do not publish an unverified address |

All media must have usage permission and descriptive alt text. AI-generated or stock images should be used as illustrative visuals only and must not be presented as completed My Space projects.

## 24. Final Content Recommendation

The website content should make the visitor feel that My Space is prepared for the questions that come before a project begins:

> **What should I do first? What will I receive? Who will coordinate it? How will I see the result? What should I prepare? What are the limitations?**

The strongest content model is:

> **Audience concern → Service explanation → Process → Visual proof → Preparation checklist → Relevant CTA.**

This structure keeps the website useful for people, aligned with the keyword strategy, and credible for a construction, design, and valuation brand.
